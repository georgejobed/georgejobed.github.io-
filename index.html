    01 — About

    Curious about life at the edges

    I am a biologist and MSc student in Natural Resources Management – Biology at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), supported by a NORSTIP scholarship. My experience spans microbiology, microbial ecology, molecular biology and environmental research — most of it carried out in the semi-arid landscapes of central Tanzania.

    I am particularly interested in how microbial diversity and function interact with environmental conditions, soil properties and ecosystem processes, especially in drylands and other extreme environments. Alongside research, I work closely with communities, training smallholder farmers in soil health, and I have taught microbiology and parasitology to undergraduate students.

    02 — Research

    What I work on

    🐦‍⬛

    MSc research · NTNU

    Zoonotic & antimicrobial-resistant bacteria in invasive house crows

    Exploring the ecological and human-related factors linked to zoonotic and antimicrobial-resistant bacteria in invasive Indian House Crows (Corvus splendens) in Dodoma Urban, Tanzania — a One Health question at the interface of wildlife, people and the environment.

    Supervisors: Prof. Augustine Arukwe, Prof. Eivin Røskaft, Dr Peter Ranke & Dr Ignas Safari

    🌱

    MKARUN · Penn State / UDOM / CDEO

    Soil health & nitrogen-fixing microbes in semi-arid Tanzania

    Studying root-nodule microbiomes of drought-tolerant legumes on smallholder farms in Nghumbi using 16S and nifH amplicon sequencing, and building a culture collection of nitrogen-fixing bacteria and other endophytes.

    Research Assistant & Project Team Member · 2023 – present

    🌿

    Bioprospecting · UDOM

    Antimicrobial activity of Moringa oleifera

    Testing crude seed and leaf extracts of Moringa oleifera against Escherichia coli, Salmonella Typhi and Candida albicans — exploring plants as sources of new antimicrobials.

    BSc research project · 2022

    🪰

    Data analysis · NTNU

    Microbial markers in wild scuttle flies

    Using discriminant analysis in R to test whether endosymbionts and other bacteria can serve as markers of sex, species, site and season in wild scuttle flies (Diptera: Phoridae).

    Manuscript in preparation

    How my research connects

    My projects sit where soils and plants, wildlife, and people meet. Microbial ecology is the thread that links them — from nitrogen-fixing bacteria that support farmers' crops to resistant bacteria moving between birds, people and the environment.

    03 — Experience

    Experience & training

    🔬Research experience

    🐦‍⬛

    Jul 2026 – present

    Ecological and Anthropogenic Correlates of Zoonotic and Antimicrobial-Resistant Bacteria in Invasive Indian House Crows (Corvus splendens) in Dodoma Urban, Tanzania

    Master's research

    Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) · Fieldwork in Dodoma, Tanzania

    I captured 53 Indian House Crows using baited ladder traps and collected their gut contents for bacterial isolation. I then isolated and identified enteric bacteria using culture and biochemical tests and assessed their antimicrobial susceptibility with the Kirby–Bauer disk diffusion method. Alongside the laboratory work, I collected environmental and anthropogenic data, which I am now analysing to understand how sampling sites and human activities relate to bacterial carriage and resistance patterns.

    Supervisors: Prof. Augustine Arukwe, Prof. Eivin Røskaft, Dr Peter Ranke & Dr Ignas Safari

    🌾

    Jul 2023 – present

    Project to Improve Soil Health and Fertility in Nghumbi (MKARUN)

    Research Assistant & Project Team Member

    Pennsylvania State University (PSU), University of Dodoma (UDOM) & Community Development and Empowerment Organization (CDEO) · Dodoma, Tanzania

    I sampled root nodules from drought-tolerant legumes on smallholder farms across two seasons, extracted DNA for 16S and nifH amplicon sequencing, and developed a culture collection of nitrogen-fixing bacteria and other endophytes using semi-selective and non-selective media. I also advise on research design and methods for Gliricidia-based agroforestry and cover crop–microbiome studies.

    Principal Investigator: Kelsey Christine Mercurio

    🌿

    Jan – May 2022

    The Antimicrobial Activities of Moringa oleifera Seed and Leaf Extracts Against Selected Pathogenic Microbes

    BSc research project

    University of Dodoma (UDOM) · Tanzania

    I prepared crude extracts from Moringa oleifera leaves and seeds by maceration and evaluated the antimicrobial activity of different concentrations against Escherichia coli, Salmonella Typhi and Candida albicans using the agar well diffusion method. I then analysed the findings and wrote a research report as part of my BSc Biology degree.

    Supervisor: Dr Mhuji Kilonzo

    University of Illinois Chicago, Center for Clinical and Translational Science · Online

    The training covered research ethics, participant eligibility and recruitment, informed consent, Institutional Review Boards, data collection and protection, and ethical field research practice.
